## One-tap sign-in

You can now log in to Vicoa with your Google or Apple account instead of an email and password.

![The Vicoa sign-in screen with Google and Apple login options](/images/updates/google-apple-sign-in-1.png)

## Existing accounts link automatically

If your Google or Apple account uses the same email address as your existing Vicoa account, the
two are linked on first sign-in. Your machines, sessions, and subscription carry over — there is
nothing to migrate.
