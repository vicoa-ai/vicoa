## One link, a live view

Open the **Share** button in a session's header, or **Share** in its **⋯** menu, and
press **Create link**. The link is copied for you. Anyone who opens it sees the
transcript exactly as it looks in Vicoa, and keeps seeing new messages as the agent
works, until you revoke the link.

A project shares the same way, as **one** link: tick **Tasks**, **Sessions**, or
both under *What this link shares*. The page puts the tasks on the sidebar's Tasks
row and the sessions underneath it, and new tasks and sessions show up as they
happen.

## You decide who reads it

- **Anyone with the link**, no account needed, or **Vicoa users only**.
- **Expiry**: never, or 1, 7, 30, or 90 days. Revoke any link at any time; it stops
  working for everyone immediately.
- **Each half narrows on its own**: tasks by status or label, sessions by date range.
  A link carrying tasks can also let signed-in Vicoa users comment on them under their
  own name.

## Nothing about you unless you say so

A shared page shows the work and nothing else: no name, no avatar, no branch names;
wherever you appear you are "Owner". Two switches, **My name and avatar** and
**Branch names**, turn those on for a link when you want them.

## What to check before you share

The page shows the full transcript, including any file contents or credentials the
agent printed. Vicoa does not redact transcripts, so read what the agent saw before
you hand out the link.
