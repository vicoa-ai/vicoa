## Share with the people you work with

A [share link](/updates/share-links) is for anyone who has it. Now you can also give
specific people access. Open **Share** on a project or a session and switch to the
**People** tab.

Enter an email address, pick a role, choose whether they see the **Tasks**, the
**Sessions** or both, and press **Invite**. The project shows up in their sidebar under
**Shared with me**. If they don't have a Vicoa account yet, the access waits for them and
attaches when they sign up with that address.

## Four roles

- **Viewer** reads the task board and the shared sessions
- **Commenter** can also comment and react on tasks
- **Editor** can also create and edit tasks
- **Admin** can also manage who has access

Change someone's role, narrow what they see, or remove them at any time. Changes apply
immediately.

## Teams

Create a team in **Settings → Teams** and invite people by email or with an invite link.
An invitation shows up in the invitee's account menu with **Join** and **Decline**.
Share a project with the team and everyone on it gets access; people who leave the team
lose it.

## What they see

People you share a session with see the conversation, without the terminal, the files,
or anything else that reaches your machine. With a project selected, the Tasks page shows
who it is shared with next to **Share**.

Available now in the web app and the desktop app. Read more in the
[features guide](/docs/vicoa-features#share-with-people-and-teams).
